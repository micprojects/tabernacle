Website favicons used to identify supported sites in Site Enhancements.

These are the sites’ original favicon files. Their respective owners retain all
rights to their logos and marks; they are not Tabernacle artwork. Sources and
retrieval details are recorded in sources.json.

The icons are bundled with the extension so the settings page does not need
network requests or site-data permissions to display them. The GitHub favicon
is its official light version, shown on a white badge in either colour mode.
