acorn.svg and folder-icons.svg combine the original paths into SVG symbols. Each official symbol's name
matches icons/desktop/<size>/<name>.svg at the revision above. Original path
geometry, fill rules and native view boxes are preserved. Firefox's privileged
context-fill/context-fill-opacity attributes are omitted so the inline SVG
can inherit currentColor and work in ordinary extension pages.

The following Tabernacle additions and adaptations are not official Acorn assets:
- folder-open-16 adds a slanted front panel for expanded folders, matching
  folder-16's tab shape, rounded corners and 1.5px outline weight.
- folder-add-16 combines folder-16 with an unscaled add-12 translated by (6, 6).
  A clip leaves space at the folder's lower right for the plus.
- add-circle-12 converts add-circle-fill-12 to an outlined circle, preserving
  its plus glyph. subtract-circle-12 uses the same circle and a horizontal
  bar with the same 1.25px weight and rounded ends.
- letter-a-16 through letter-z-16 and number-0-16 through number-9-16 are original
  Tabernacle drawings, released under the project's MIT licence (root LICENSE).
  They use native 16px view boxes, centred characters, 1.5px strokes and rounded
  ends and joins to sit alongside the Acorn icons. The characters are SVG paths,
  with no font dependency; these are not official Acorn assets.

All UI icons render at their native 12, 16 or 20 CSS pixel size. The registry
in ../../src/icons.js maps Tabernacle actions to these symbols. The existing
Firefox container artwork and Tabernacle extension logo are separate assets.

folder-icons.svg contains 72 unmodified 16px Acorn symbols followed by 36 custom
letter and number symbols for folder appearance choices.
../../src/folder-appearance.js lists their labels, categories and search
keywords; ../../src/folder-icons.js renders them at their native size.
