The SVG files in this directory are Firefox container icons from Mozilla's
browser/components/contextualidentity/content directory. They include explicit
fill fallbacks so the icons render outside Firefox's privileged browser UI.

Source: https://github.com/mozilla-firefox/firefox/tree/main/browser/components/contextualidentity/content
